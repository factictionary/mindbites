🛡️ 1. Overview & Principles

The Privacy-First Standard: MindBites does not require an account, email address, password, or sign-up. We do not track your reading habits on remote servers. Live news summaries and audio digest synthesis execute directly on your device when you open the app, ensuring 0% background battery drain and 100% data confidentiality.

This Privacy Policy explains how MindBites ("we", "us", "our") manages information across our mobile applications. By using MindBites, you acknowledge the privacy practices detailed below.

📰 3. News Aggregation & RSS Feeds

MindBites features an on-demand, 2-Minute Morning Snapshot and Full RSS News Reader powered by real-time public RSS syndication:

  • Syndicated Public Feeds: News headlines and excerpt metadata are fetched from publicly accessible RSS feeds published by BBC News, The Guardian, The New York Times, The Verge, TechCrunch, CNBC, ScienceDaily, NASA, and Yahoo Finance.
  • No Background Daemon: The application does not run background cron jobs or continuous polling daemons. RSS feeds are requested purely upon app launch when the user requests a briefing.
  • No User Profiling: We do not log which specific articles or topics you read, click, or listen to.
  • Direct Publisher Links: When you tap a story or source badge, your device opens the publisher's original article in your external web browser. Your interaction with the external website is governed by the respective publisher's privacy policy.

☀️ 4. Weather Forecast Coordinates

To display morning weather summaries (temperature and condition) on the Snapshot Primer card, MindBites makes lightweight API requests to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com):

  • No continuous GPS tracking is performed.
  • Requests use generalized coordinates to retrieve regional meteorological conditions without collecting personal identification.
  • Open-Meteo operates under open non-commercial guidelines and does not log personal user profiles.

💾 5. On-Device Storage (Hive & Cache)

MindBites uses Hive and on-device cache storage strictly within your device's private app sandbox to persist:

  • snapshot_cache: Cached latest snapshot edition (auto-expires hourly) to allow instant cold starts and offline reading.
  • subscription_box: Local verification token for PRO/VIP ad-free status (including 7-day free trial status).
  • favorites_box: Your saved bookmarks and liked quotes.
  • progress_box: Read card IDs and streak counters.
  • image_cache: Temporary cached fact card graphics for seamless high-resolution social sharing.

You can erase all locally stored data at any time by clearing app data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

👑 6. In-App Subscriptions & Advertising

Ad-Free Experience for PRO Users: Upgrading to MindBites PRO via Google Play In-App Purchases (including our 7-Day Free Trial on the Annual Plan) completely removes and suppresses all advertising SDKs (no banner ads, no native ads, no interstitial ads).

Google AdMob for Free Users: In the free tier, banner (refreshed per card swipe) and optional rewarded ads are served via Google AdMob. Google may use standard anonymous advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

You may reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking anytime in your Android OS Settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

👶 8. Children's Privacy

MindBites is an educational knowledge app suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because our app operates without accounts, zero personal data is gathered by design.

⚖️ 9. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under GDPR (EEA/UK) and CCPA/CPRA (California), users have the right to access, delete, and control their data:

  • Zero Remote Data: Because we do not store personal data on remote servers, no personal profile exists to sell, share, or disclose.
  • Data Deletion: Simply reset your stats inside the app or uninstall the application to permanently erase all local device history.
